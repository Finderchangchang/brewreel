# 美业简报模板

通用字段同 `industries/food/brief-template.md` 开头所列。

## 本行业专属字段
storeName、city、area、subVertical（hair/nail/lash/skincare）、services[]{id,name,price,unit,durationMin,includes,excludes,from}、addOns[]{cond,extra}、deal{price,comparePrice,basis,evidence}、photos[]{src,type(作品|前后对比),merchantShot,retouched,aiGenerated}、beforeAfterSets[]{before,after,consent,retouched,sameAngle}、artist{name,years,skills[]}、badges[]、products{brand,type,specialCosmeticRegChecked}、hours、licenses{business,hygiene,healthCerts}、audience、concerns[]、platform、attachDeal、prepaidCard{rule,contractSigned,refundable}、ownerSaid[]、durationSecs。
