# General (no industry pack)

This is not a seventh industry pack. Use it when the product is outside software, food, ecommerce, education, beauty, and travel. Validation runs only the shared advertising-law rules in industries/_base. A passing validation is not industry compliance.

Borrow the closest industry recipe for layout and say which one you borrowed. Borrowed layout is not that industry's full review.

enabledShots is a recommendation, not a whitelist. This pack does not reopen beforeAfter (_base still disables it). steps, meter, storeCard, mockApp, and photoShot are all allowed.

Demonstrate the core action the way the borrowed recipe does. This pack does not require a photo and does not ban mockApp.

Driver-assistance wording, drugs, medical devices, and finance must be reviewed by a qualified person. Do not say the video is already compliant.
