# journey style · spec

> Status: **stable**. Anonymized summary of a breakdown of a character-journey short video (4:5, about 30 s). No reference footage, stills, characters, brands or URLs are in this repo; the mascot, city, signs and sample copy are all original to this project. The Chinese `STYLE.md` has the full tables; `recipes.md` (Chinese) lists every field a model fills, beat by beat.

A mascot (a round red-panda cub on an electric-blue hover board) crosses a flat-illustrated city left to right in one continuous take. Each district is a content category; an overhead billboard pops up that category's representative title; the sky goes from day to night; at the end the whole city zooms into a product screen with a brand card and a stat pill.

1. **Basics**: 4:5 (1080×1350) by default, 9:16 supported; 30 fps; 120 BPM. No cuts except the final zoom. Each district is 8 beats (4 s). No voice-over: speech bubbles and sound words only. Typical length: hook 2 s + 6 districts 24 s + finale 4 s = 30 s.
2. **Structure**: `opening` (4 beats) → `district` × 4–6 (8 beats each) → `finale` (8–10 beats: stop + counter, 0.3 s zoom, end card). Inside a district: beat 0 a category-colored arrow sign (with a "k / n" badge) passes the mascot and the category pill switches; at beat 0.3 the billboard rises from behind the buildings and pops its title as it rises; the text box is clamped inside the safe zone (x 180–900 in 9:16) and stays fully readable until beat 7.2; beats 3–6 the gag: the prop reaches the mascot exactly on its beat, the camera slows to 0.52–0.7×, the mascot changes pose, a sound word with SFX, then a bubble; beat 7.2 the billboard folds and sinks and the next one follows right after; beats 6–7 back to cruise. One backdrop per film (`opening.params.skyline`): `modern` city (default), `street` (low-rise lanes), `oldtown` (hills and a pagoda, white walls with dark tile roofs, willows, stone paving and a canal); old-town / ancient-town travel storyboards must use `oldtown`. Fourteen district types (`scene`), each an illustrated district plus one gag, each declaring the backdrops it fits (`tokens.json` `scenes.*.fits`): `stack` (docs street; hop over a block tower), `launch` (countdown, the rocket lifts off in front of the mascot, soot face), `factory` (robot arm dusts the mascot), `studio` (two camera flashes, cheer), `observatory` (data street, bars grow; robot bird flies along), `neon` (night street whose rooftop signs show this stop's category and tag; glitching neon billboard; last stop only), `phone` (phone-shaped tower with a list UI; a receipt is scanned into a ticked row), `home` (apartments whose windows light up, a heart-window house; hearts float over), `cafe` (giant cup on the roof; a coffee is handed over), `market` (four striped stalls; catch a gift box; fits every backdrop), and the old-town set `gate` (city wall and gate tower; photo flashes), `bridge` (three-arch stone bridge over the canal, a boat; a swallow flies along), `teahouse` (two-storey wooden teahouse, tea banner, indigo-dyed cloth; a cup of tea is handed over), `lantern` (archway and strings of red lanterns that light up left to right; last stop only). Adjacent districts must differ and the props must match the stop's content.
3. **Visual system**: flat fills, one ink outline `#1F191A` (mascot 5px, near buildings and props 4px, none mid-ground); only the sky uses a gradient. The city (`art/`): sky → far skyline → pale mid towers with themed silhouettes → themed near districts → street (sidewalk + asphalt with yellow dashes; grass below in 9:16; `oldtown` swaps in hills, white-wall silhouettes, old houses and a stone street with a canal). Each district has one main color shared by the category pill, billboard band, sign and end chips. The opening pad is a neutral striped pedestal (a stone pier in the old town), with no subject-specific props. Sky progress is automatic: last district night, second-to-last dusk, third-to-last warm (5+ districts), others day; windows, street lamps, sign up-lights and neon follow it. Billboard: category-colored top band (dot + category + optional tag), white body with a two-line title and an optional source line, one thick post with a cross bar and two up-lights. Type sizes (1080 wide, raised to our floors): hook 124 (only outlined headline), kicker 48, billboard title 46 (min 40), band text 28, source 26, sign 44, category pill 34, bubble 40, sound word 46, counter 76/40, slogan 48, CTA 40. 11 hard-cut story expressions mapped to art poses in `parts/rider.tsx`; we do not use the reference's paper-character-plus-paper-plane pairing. `themes` (`day-city`, `mint-town`) only color the UI and end card; city and mascot colors are overridden through the `art` key in `tokens.json`.
4. **Camera**: mascot screen-locked at 35% from the left, floating ±16px / 2 s; parallax far 0.12, mid 0.55, billboards 0.42 (slow, so the larger text can be read), near 1.0. Camera x(t) is the integral of speed: idle → takeoff → cruise, per-district gag slowdowns, 1.3 s stop in the finale. Each district gets its own cruise speed so the illustrated districts (2200 world units × `layout.*.cityScale`: 0.9 in 4:5, 1.0 in 9:16) tile exactly, about 550–610 px/s. World positions are back-solved from times (`parts/plan.ts`): sign at beat 0, billboard centered at 60% at beat 2.5 (then clamped into the safe zone), prop at the gag beat; the whole city is shifted so the launch rocket sits in front of the mascot on its gag beat. The only scale change: the world zooms to 0.72 into a browser frame (address bar shows the product name, never a URL).
5. **Motion**: hook already on screen at frame 0 (0.86×) and springs to 1 in 0.17 s; billboard rises at beat 0.3, pops text 0.12 s later in about 0.22 s, holds to beat 7.2, folds in 0.12 s; world text fades at the edges of the card zone (no half-cut text); bubble in 0.1 s, hold from 0.85 s by length; sound word in 0.07 s with overshoot, hold 0.42 s; category pill swap 0.13 s; neon glitch 1.6 beats of offset, RGB split and flicker; counter 1.0 s ease-out; brand card spring.
6. **Components**: `film.tsx` (JourneyFilm: city back layer → billboards → city front layer → props → signs → mascot → bubbles → UI → finale), `parts/plan.ts` (world script, scene → art district mapping, sky progress, per-district speed), `art/` (mascot `Mascot`, city `CityWorld`; see `art/README.md`), `parts/props.tsx` (gag props, robot bird / swallow, handed cup, scanning phone, floating hearts, opening pad), `parts/ui.tsx` (hook, HUD, billboard incl. neon mode, sign, bubble, sound word), `parts/outro.tsx` (counter, browser frame and content wall, brand card, chips, stat pill, fireworks, confetti), `parts/rider.tsx` (mascot adapter).
7. **Sound**: on-beat ticks for signs; every sound word has one SFX on the off-beat (`scenes.*.sfx`, in beats); bubbles have none. Opening pop + takeoff whoosh; finale ticks, ding, zoom swish, brand pop.
8. **Fixed vs filled**: fixed outline, UI band, billboard and sign shapes, parallax, speed curve, 8 beats per district, sky direction, end card. The model fills: hook number and kicker; per district category, title, scene (optional tag, source, bubble, sound word); finale slogan, CTA, 1–2 stats.
9. **Rules** (all enforced): spec limits (headline ≤6, kicker ≤10, category ≤6, title ≤20, tag ≤6, source ≤14, bubble ≤10, sound word ≤5, slogan ≤12); `rules.json` (16–40 s, 5–8 shots, opening → districts → finale); `checks.mjs` J1–J17 (4–6 districts, adjacent scenes differ, unique categories, headline has a number, finale brand/CTA copy `meta`, stats appear in `meta.facts`, districts between opening and finale, every price/time such as "N 元", "HH:MM", "N 小时" appears verbatim in `meta.facts`, headline numbers equal the district count or appear in `meta.facts`, warn when a night scene is not the last district, scenes must fit the backdrop, old-town travel must use `oldtown`, warn on truncated category names, copied hook patterns such as "5 个账本街", long titles with no pause, a finale with neither stats nor CTA; dates in notices must appear in `meta.facts`). Industry rules still apply; a promotion-word error names the word and asks to delete it unless the promotion is real (column names like "今日精选" no longer count). Self-check: the `district` check frame is taken at beat 3.2 (spec `checkBeat`), where the billboard title and category must be visible (spec `mustShow`). The city background SVG carries `data-probe-skip`; the neon rooftop signs take their words from the storyboard.

**9:16**: `tokens.layout["9:16"]` moves the UI band to y 272, billboard to y 400–676 and 680 wide (text clamped to x 180–900), mascot to y 968 (1.15×), horizon to y 1580 with road and grass below (a canal in the old town), city scale 1.0. Finale: browser frame y 284–1150, stat card right below at y 1170–1306, and a text-free little skyline fills the platform-UI area underneath.

**Fits**: products with many clearly separated categories (content platforms, feature-rich software, multi-store or multi-category businesses, course catalogs, a sightseeing route). **Does not fit**: single-benefit or process-heavy products (use cards).
