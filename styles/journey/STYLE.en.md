# journey style · spec

> Status: **stable**. What we learned from a genre of character-journey short videos is the **skeleton** (one continuous sideways take, layered parallax, one category per stop, one small gag per stop, time moving forward, slow-downs used as close-ups). The **skin is entirely this project's own**: a "travel stationery" set — a ticket across the top, a postcard for every stop, a split-flap hook, and the same ticket punched and flipped over at the end — in a post-green / graphite / lime palette. No reference footage, stills, characters, brands or URLs are in this repo. How each element differs from the reference and how the colors were measured: [`originality.md`](originality.md) (Chinese). The Chinese `STYLE.md` has the full tables; `recipes.md` (Chinese) lists every field a model fills, beat by beat.

A mascot (see `template/src/styles/journey/art/README.md`) rides a hover board left to right through a flat-illustrated city in one continuous take. A ticket across the top shows the whole route; at each stop the ticket's current stop switches, and an airmail-bordered postcard is tossed in from the right with that category's representative title; when the stop ends the postcard is "mailed" and shrinks into that stop on the ticket. The sky goes from day to night; at the terminus the city turns into a warm-lit night scene (edges darkened but still in colour, a warm lamp glow behind the ticket), the ticket from the top slides to the centre and unfolds into a large ticket (the whole route travelled), and the mascot hops onto its top edge; if there is a number, one running total ("1200 episodes") is printed next to the finish flag; a ticket clipper punches a hole in the stub, the ticket flips over, and the back shows only "last stop", the product name, the slogan and the CTA; the mascot then rides off the right edge of the frame, leaving just the ticket.

1. **Basics**: 4:5 (1080×1350) by default, 9:16 supported; 30 fps; 120 BPM. No cuts at all (the finale does not zoom or switch screens). Each district is 8 beats (4 s). No voice-over by default: speech bubbles and sound words only. With `meta.voice`, each stop can have one spoken line (`vo`); shots are retimed to the voice (districts 3.5–5 s instead of a fixed 8 beats) and a one-line subtitle strip under the mascot (under the sign in 4:5) lights up word by word (see "有配音时" in `recipes.md`). Typical length: hook 2 s + 6 districts 24 s + finale 5 s = 31 s.
2. **Structure**: `opening` (4 beats) → `district` × 4–6 (8 beats each) → `finale` (8–10 beats: stop at the terminus, the ticket slides down and unfolds, a running total is printed by the finish flag, the ticket gets punched, flips to show product name + slogan, the mascot rides off). Inside a district: beat 0 a category-colored arrow sign (with a "k / n" badge) passes the mascot and the ticket's current stop switches (stop number + name), while a lime pin keeps moving along the route; at beat 0.3 the postcard is tossed in from the right (spring + rotation) and its text appears 0.1 s later (category + tag, title, source; the stamp shows the stop number); the text stays in the safe zone (x 180–900 in 9:16) and fully readable until beat 7.1; beats 3–6 the gag: the prop reaches the mascot exactly on its beat, the camera slows to 0.52–0.7×, the mascot changes pose, a sound word with SFX, then a bubble; beat 7.1 the postcard is mailed into its stop on the ticket; beats 6–7 back to cruise. One backdrop per film (`opening.params.skyline`): `modern` city (default), `street` (low-rise lanes), `oldtown` (hills and a pagoda, white walls with dark tile roofs, willows, stone paving and a canal); old-town travel storyboards must use `oldtown`. Fourteen district types (`scene`), each an illustrated district plus one gag from this project's own station / mail set (gag kinds rotate: obstacle / help / show / buddy / light-up / catch), each declaring the backdrops it fits (`tokens.json` `scenes.*.fits`): `crossing` (a crossing gate lowers and the mascot limbos under it), `postbox` (a postbox building spits a perforated stamp sticker onto the board), `punch` (a ticket clipper punches the ticket the mascot holds up), `booth` (a photo booth prints a three-shot strip, no screen flash), `hitch` (a hedgehog with a suitcase hops on for a ride), `platform` (platform lamps light up one by one; last stop only), `phone`, `home`, `cafe`, `market` (fits every backdrop), and the old-town set `gate` (a wooden seal stamps a travel pass), `bridge` (a koi leaps past the mascot), `teahouse`, `lantern` (last stop only). Adjacent districts must differ and the props must match the stop's content.
3. **Visual system (our own skin)**: UI and finale use the `post-green` theme — graphite outline `#23262E`, post green `#1B6B2F` (ticket stub, the travelled part of the route, the finale clipper, the product name on the ticket back), lime `#7CFC3A` (route pin, split-flap digits, highlighter strokes), pale lake-cyan paper `#C6E1E7` (the theme background; since v0.3.0 the finale no longer lays it over the city as a veil). The demo theme `plum-ticket` swaps in a wine stub `#7A1030` with moss `#4B760A` on cool grey. Every chromatic theme color is at least CIEDE2000 20 away from the reference palette (numbers in `originality.md`). The city (`art/`) has one main color per district; signs, the postcard's stamp and airmail stripes, and travelled ticket stops use it. Prop colors (`scenes.*.props`) are slots — `main / deep / mid / accent / white / ink` — resolved from the district's art palette so buildings and props share one family. Sky progress is automatic: last district night, second-to-last dusk, third-to-last warm (5+ districts), others day. **Ticket** (info layer): a full-width ticket at the top (y 112 in 4:5, y 272 in 9:16): route on the left (start dot → stops → finish flag; travelled part solid green, the rest dashed; a lime pin moves continuously), "stop number + stop name" above the current stop, a green stub on the right with the product name, a perforation with a half-circle notch top and bottom. **Postcard** (per-stop carrier): airmail diagonal-stripe border (district color + brand color + white), paper inside; category + outlined tag at top left, a two-line title, a short rule + source at the bottom; a perforated stamp at top right (district color, stop number, a dashed postmark and two wavy cancel lines). Alternating slight tilt; at night the paper only warms up (no neon or glitch mode). **Hook**: split-flap board — one dark flap tile per character (center seam, side pins), digits highlighted; the kicker is a bold line with a highlighter stroke. **Finale**: a ticket check at the terminus — the carrier is the very ticket that sat at the top all film, so nothing new appears. Light: no grey veil; a warm-lit night instead — a multiply vignette (centre untouched, edges pushed toward petrol blue, buildings and lit windows keep their colour) plus a screen-blended warm glow (the window-lamp yellow) just behind the ticket, so the ticket is the brightest thing on screen. Front: the ticket slides to the centre and unfolds; the whole route is travelled, stop numbers under the stops, "start / end" at the two ends, "Route complete" at top left, product name on the stub; with a number, **one** running total is printed above the finish flag at top right ("1200 episodes": number in post green 46, unit in ink 30, source 26 on the next line) with a dashed line down to the flag — it is the tally of the route, not a separate stat card. During the flip, the frames where the ticket is edge-on show an outlined paper edge (its thickness), so no frame is empty. A ticket clipper reaches in from the upper right and punches a real hole in the lower stub (a mask, so the city shows through), and one stub-colored chad tumbles down. After the flip: the stub is on the left, reads "USED", and still has the hole; the main area has a thin printed frame, a small flag + "Last stop", the product name (large, post green), a dashed rule, the slogan and the highlighted CTA, vertically centred — **no numbers on the back**. The mascot stands on the ticket's top edge, points at the running total, hops during the flip, then crouches and rides off the right edge of the frame (this leg of the trip is over), so the final frames show only the ticket. No stamp grid, category color blocks, side-by-side numbers, confetti, goodbye gesture or goodbye bubble. Layered paper-cut look: far, middle and near layers and the mascot are each a sheet of paper casting a hard paper shadow offset to the lower right (darker when nearer, weaker at night); the sky is five stacked sheets with wavy top edges, windows are holes cut into the wall. Graphite outlines; UI pieces use hard offset shadows in the outline color. Type sizes (1080 wide, all above our floors): split-flap 116 (min 64), kicker 48, ticket stop 34 / number 26 / stub 30, postcard title 46 (min 40), category 32, tag / source 26, stamp number 34, sign 44 / badge 30, bubble 40, sound word 46, finale front "Route complete" 32 / stop numbers 26, back "Last stop" 28 / product name 92 (min 52) / stub "USED" 32, slogan 48, CTA 40, front running total 46 (shrinks to 0.72× at most) + unit 30 / source 26. 11 hard-cut story expressions mapped to art poses in `parts/rider.tsx`. `themes` only color the UI and finale; city and mascot colors are overridden through the `art` key in `tokens.json`.
4. **Camera**: mascot screen-locked at 35% from the left, floating ±16px / 2 s; parallax far 0.12, mid 0.55, near 1.0. The ticket and postcards are screen-space, so a title stays in one place from reveal to mailing (6.8 beats of reading time). Camera x(t) is the integral of speed: idle → takeoff → cruise, per-district gag slowdowns, 1.3 s stop in the finale. Each district gets its own cruise speed so the illustrated districts (2200 world units × `layout.*.cityScale`: 0.9 in 4:5, 1.0 in 9:16) tile exactly. World positions are back-solved from times (`parts/plan.ts`): sign at beat 0, prop at the gag beat; the city is shifted so the postbox building sits in front of the mascot on its gag beat. No scale change at the end: the city stops and turns into a warm-lit night, the top ticket slides down and unfolds, the mascot hops onto its top edge facing right, and after the flip it rides off the right edge (the camera does not follow).
5. **Motion**: split-flap already on screen at frame 0 (0.9×), springs to 1; from 0.22 s each tile flips once (0.05 s apart, 0.14 s each); exits by flipping up tile by tile. The ticket is not on screen while the split-flap hook is up (this keeps the top third light); it slides in from the top edge in 0.3 s at 90% of the hook, as the tiles leave. Ticket stop name slides up in 0.18 s on each switch. Postcard tossed in at beat 0.3, text 0.1 s later, gentle float, mailed in 0.36 s at beat 7.1. World text fades at the edges of the card zone (no half-cut text). Bubble in 0.1 s, hold from 0.85 s by length; sound word (a scalloped postmark badge with a dashed inner ring) in 0.07 s with overshoot and a hard shadow, hold 0.42 s; night-platform lamps light one every 0.3 beats. Finale: from 0 s the top ticket slides to the centre and unfolds in 0.55 s while the city turns into the warm-lit night in 0.45 s (no empty frame in between), the mascot hops onto the ticket from 0.1 s; the running total is typed out left to right from 0.6 s over 0.4 s (no count-up, no enlargement) while its dashed line draws down to the flag; the clipper reaches in from 0.95 s and closes at 1.25 s (hole + chad); the ticket flips around its vertical axis from 1.65 s for 0.5 s (with perspective and a visible paper edge; the mascot hops); slogan at 2.2 s, CTA at 2.35 s; at 2.35 s the mascot crouches for 0.14 s and rides off to the right in 0.65 s (nose up 9°, speed lines), leaving about 2 s of the ticket alone.
6. **Components**: `film.tsx` (JourneyFilm: city back → city front → pad → props → signs → finale ticket (with clipper and chad) → mascot → gag effects → bubbles → postcards → split-flap hook → ticket), `parts/plan.ts` (world script, scene → art district mapping, prop color slots, sky progress, per-district speed), `art/` (mascot, city; see `art/README.md`), `parts/props.tsx` (gag props: `Props` in the world, `GagFx` following the mascot, `StartPad`), `parts/ui.tsx` (`HookTitle`, `RouteTicket`, `Postcard` incl. stamp, sign, bubble, sound word), `parts/outro.tsx` (finale ticket `Finale`: warm night light (multiply vignette + screen glow), front unfolded from the top ticket with the running total by the finish flag, back with product name, slogan and CTA (no numbers), paper edge during the flip, clipper, masked hole and chad; `finTicketRect` for mascot placement, `finaleTimes` for the step timings, `finaleExpr` for finale expressions; the ride-off path is in `film.tsx`), `parts/rider.tsx` (mascot adapter).
7. **Sound**: on-beat ticks for signs and ticket switches; every sound word has one SFX on the off-beat (`scenes.*.sfx`, in beats); bubbles have none. Opening: two soft flap ticks + takeoff whoosh. Finale: ticket swish, two print ticks for the running total (when there is one), punch tap, flip swish, ding when the back settles, whoosh as the mascot rides off. Optional voice-over: each line starts 0.15 s after its shot begins; the music ducks 10 dB under speech (120 ms down, 300 ms back) and is unchanged elsewhere.
8. **Fixed vs filled**: fixed outline, ticket / postcard / stamp shapes, parallax, speed curve, 8 beats per district, sky direction, the ending. The model fills: hook number and kicker; per district category, title, scene (optional tag, source, bubble, sound word); finale slogan, CTA, at most 1 stat.
9. **Rules** (all enforced): spec limits (headline ≤6, kicker ≤10, category ≤6, title ≤20, tag ≤6, source ≤14, bubble ≤10, sound word ≤5, slogan ≤12; at most 1 finale stat); `rules.json` (16–40 s, 5–8 shots, opening → districts → finale); `checks.mjs` J1–J18 (4–6 districts, adjacent scenes differ, unique categories, headline has a number, finale brand/CTA copy `meta`, stats appear in `meta.facts`, districts between opening and finale, every price/time appears verbatim in `meta.facts`, headline numbers equal the district count or appear in `meta.facts`, night scenes last, scenes fit the backdrop, old-town travel uses `oldtown`, truncated category names, copied hook patterns, long titles with no pause, a finale with neither stats nor CTA, dates in notices must appear in `meta.facts`, and J18: warn when the kicker reuses the "跟 X 一口气飞越 / 逛完" formula). Industry rules still apply. Self-check: the `district` check frame is taken at beat 3.2 (spec `checkBeat`), where the postcard title and category must be visible (spec `mustShow`); the ticket only names the current stop, never the next one. The city background SVG carries `data-probe-skip`.

**9:16**: `tokens.layout["9:16"]` puts the ticket at y 272, postcards at y 398–674 and 700 wide (text inside x 180–900), mascot at y 968 (1.15×), horizon at y 1580 with road and grass below (a canal in the old town), city scale 1.0. During the hook the ticket is not shown yet: kicker at y 380, split-flap at y 470. The finale ticket starts at y 700 (500–560 tall) and the mascot stands on its top edge (head at about y 390). 4:5 values are in `layout["4:5"]` (kicker y 160, split-flap y 244, finale ticket from y 450); the 4:5 frame is short, so `layout["4:5"].sky` puts the sun (y ≈ 520) and the clouds (y 460–570) below the split-flap, leaving only the notice, kicker and split-flap in the top third.

**Fits**: products with many clearly separated categories (content platforms, feature-rich software, multi-store or multi-category businesses, course catalogs, a sightseeing route). **Does not fit**: single-benefit or process-heavy products (use cards).
