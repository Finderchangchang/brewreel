# journey style · spec

> Status: **stable**. What we learned from a genre of character-journey short videos is the **skeleton** (one continuous sideways take, layered parallax, one category per stop, one small gag per stop, time moving forward, slow-downs used as close-ups). The **skin is entirely this project's own**: a "travel stationery" set — a ticket across the top, a postcard for every stop, a split-flap hook, and a stamp card at the end — in a post-green / graphite / lime palette. No reference footage, stills, characters, brands or URLs are in this repo. How each element differs from the reference and how the colors were measured: [`originality.md`](originality.md) (Chinese). The Chinese `STYLE.md` has the full tables; `recipes.md` (Chinese) lists every field a model fills, beat by beat.

A mascot (see `template/src/styles/journey/art/README.md`) rides a hover board left to right through a flat-illustrated city in one continuous take. A ticket across the top shows the whole route; at each stop the ticket's current stop switches, and an airmail-bordered postcard is tossed in from the right with that category's representative title; when the stop ends the postcard is "mailed" and shrinks into that stop on the ticket. The sky goes from day to night; at the terminus the city dims, a stamp card rises, every stop gets stamped in order, and a final "arrived" seal lands.

1. **Basics**: 4:5 (1080×1350) by default, 9:16 supported; 30 fps; 120 BPM. No cuts at all (the finale does not zoom or switch screens). Each district is 8 beats (4 s). No voice-over: speech bubbles and sound words only. Typical length: hook 2 s + 6 districts 24 s + finale 5 s = 31 s.
2. **Structure**: `opening` (4 beats) → `district` × 4–6 (8 beats each) → `finale` (8–10 beats: stop at the terminus, stamp card rises, stops stamped one by one, stats count up, "arrived" seal + slogan). Inside a district: beat 0 a category-colored arrow sign (with a "k / n" badge) passes the mascot and the ticket's current stop switches (stop number + name), while a lime pin keeps moving along the route; at beat 0.3 the postcard is tossed in from the right (spring + rotation) and its text appears 0.1 s later (category + tag, title, source; the stamp shows the stop number); the text stays in the safe zone (x 180–900 in 9:16) and fully readable until beat 7.1; beats 3–6 the gag: the prop reaches the mascot exactly on its beat, the camera slows to 0.52–0.7×, the mascot changes pose, a sound word with SFX, then a bubble; beat 7.1 the postcard is mailed into its stop on the ticket; beats 6–7 back to cruise. One backdrop per film (`opening.params.skyline`): `modern` city (default), `street` (low-rise lanes), `oldtown` (hills and a pagoda, white walls with dark tile roofs, willows, stone paving and a canal); old-town travel storyboards must use `oldtown`. Fourteen district types (`scene`), each an illustrated district plus one gag from this project's own station / mail set (gag kinds rotate: obstacle / help / show / buddy / light-up / catch), each declaring the backdrops it fits (`tokens.json` `scenes.*.fits`): `crossing` (a crossing gate lowers and the mascot limbos under it), `postbox` (a postbox building spits a perforated stamp sticker onto the board), `punch` (a ticket clipper punches the ticket the mascot holds up), `booth` (a photo booth prints a three-shot strip, no screen flash), `hitch` (a hedgehog with a suitcase hops on for a ride), `platform` (platform lamps light up one by one; last stop only), `phone`, `home`, `cafe`, `market` (fits every backdrop), and the old-town set `gate` (a wooden seal stamps a travel pass), `bridge` (a koi leaps past the mascot), `teahouse`, `lantern` (last stop only). Adjacent districts must differ and the props must match the stop's content.
3. **Visual system (our own skin)**: UI and finale use the `post-green` theme — graphite outline `#23262E`, post green `#1B6B2F` (ticket stub, stamp-card header, "arrived" seal, the travelled part of the route), lime `#7CFC3A` (route pin, split-flap digits, highlighter strokes), pale lake-cyan paper `#C6E1E7` (the finale's dimming veil). The demo theme `plum-ticket` swaps in a wine stub `#7A1030` with moss `#4B760A` on cool grey. Every chromatic theme color is at least CIEDE2000 20 away from the reference palette (numbers in `originality.md`). The city (`art/`) has one main color per district; signs, the postcard's stamp and airmail stripes, travelled ticket stops and the finale stamps all use it. Prop colors (`scenes.*.props`) are slots — `main / deep / mid / accent / white / ink` — resolved from the district's art palette so buildings and props share one family. Sky progress is automatic: last district night, second-to-last dusk, third-to-last warm (5+ districts), others day. **Ticket** (info layer): a full-width ticket at the top (y 112 in 4:5, y 272 in 9:16): route on the left (start dot → stops → finish flag; travelled part solid green, the rest dashed; a lime pin moves continuously), "stop number + stop name" above the current stop, a green stub on the right with the product name, a perforation with a half-circle notch top and bottom. **Postcard** (per-stop carrier): airmail diagonal-stripe border (district color + brand color + white), paper inside; category + outlined tag at top left, a two-line title, a short rule + source at the bottom; a perforated stamp at top right (district color, stop number, a dashed postmark and two wavy cancel lines). Alternating slight tilt; at night the paper only warms up (no neon or glitch mode). **Hook**: split-flap board — one dark flap tile per character (center seam, side pins), digits highlighted; the kicker is a bold line with a highlighter stroke. **Finale**: stamp card — green header with the product name, a grid of stop stamps (double-bordered rounded stamps in each district's color, numbered), a stats band under a dashed rule (a small "stamps k/n" badge on the left, 1–2 stats stacked as ticket lines on the right, source underneath; no side-by-side "N │ M" row), slogan and highlighted CTA, an "arrived" round seal on the header, punched-paper dots falling; the mascot lands at the card's lower-right corner facing it and says "Next ride soon!". Layered paper-cut look: far, middle and near layers and the mascot are each a sheet of paper casting a hard paper shadow offset to the lower right (darker when nearer, weaker at night); the sky is five stacked sheets with wavy top edges, windows are holes cut into the wall. Graphite outlines; UI pieces use hard offset shadows in the outline color. Type sizes (1080 wide, all above our floors): split-flap 116 (min 64), kicker 48, ticket stop 34 / number 26 / stub 30, postcard title 46 (min 40), category 32, tag / source 26, stamp number 34, sign 44 / badge 30, bubble 40, sound word 46, card brand 40, stop stamp 30 / number 26, seal 40, stats 64 / 36 / 28, slogan 48, CTA 40. 11 hard-cut story expressions mapped to art poses in `parts/rider.tsx`. `themes` only color the UI and finale; city and mascot colors are overridden through the `art` key in `tokens.json`.
4. **Camera**: mascot screen-locked at 35% from the left, floating ±16px / 2 s; parallax far 0.12, mid 0.55, near 1.0. The ticket and postcards are screen-space, so a title stays in one place from reveal to mailing (6.8 beats of reading time). Camera x(t) is the integral of speed: idle → takeoff → cruise, per-district gag slowdowns, 1.3 s stop in the finale. Each district gets its own cruise speed so the illustrated districts (2200 world units × `layout.*.cityScale`: 0.9 in 4:5, 1.0 in 9:16) tile exactly. World positions are back-solved from times (`parts/plan.ts`): sign at beat 0, prop at the gag beat; the city is shifted so the postbox building sits in front of the mascot on its gag beat. No scale change at the end: the city stops and dims, the stamp card rises from below, the mascot hops to the card's lower-right corner.
5. **Motion**: split-flap already on screen at frame 0 (0.9×), springs to 1; from 0.22 s each tile flips once (0.05 s apart, 0.14 s each); exits by flipping up tile by tile. Ticket stop name slides up in 0.18 s on each switch. Postcard tossed in at beat 0.3, text 0.1 s later, gentle float, mailed in 0.36 s at beat 7.1. World text fades at the edges of the card zone (no half-cut text). Bubble in 0.1 s, hold from 0.85 s by length; sound word (a scalloped postmark badge with a dashed inner ring) in 0.07 s with overshoot and a hard shadow, hold 0.42 s; night-platform lamps light one every 0.3 beats. Finale: dim from 0.3 s, card rises from 0.45 s (no overshoot) while the mascot hops to its corner from 0.25 s, stamps land over 0.8 s from 0.8 s (1.5× → 1 with ink dots), stats count up 0.6 s from 1.65 s, "arrived" seal at 2.35 s with slogan, CTA and punched dots.
6. **Components**: `film.tsx` (JourneyFilm: city back → city front → pad → props → signs → stamp card → mascot → gag effects → bubbles → postcards → split-flap hook → ticket), `parts/plan.ts` (world script, scene → art district mapping, prop color slots, sky progress, per-district speed), `art/` (mascot, city; see `art/README.md`), `parts/props.tsx` (gag props: `Props` in the world, `GagFx` following the mascot, `StartPad`), `parts/ui.tsx` (`HookTitle`, `RouteTicket`, `Postcard` incl. stamp, sign, bubble, sound word), `parts/outro.tsx` (stamp-card `Finale`, punched dots), `parts/rider.tsx` (mascot adapter).
7. **Sound**: on-beat ticks for signs and ticket switches; every sound word has one SFX on the off-beat (`scenes.*.sfx`, in beats); bubbles have none. Opening: two soft flap ticks + takeoff whoosh. Finale: card swish, four stamp taps, two count ticks + ding, seal pop.
8. **Fixed vs filled**: fixed outline, ticket / postcard / stamp / stamp-card shapes, parallax, speed curve, 8 beats per district, sky direction, the ending. The model fills: hook number and kicker; per district category, title, scene (optional tag, source, bubble, sound word); finale slogan, CTA, 1–2 stats.
9. **Rules** (all enforced): spec limits (headline ≤6, kicker ≤10, category ≤6, title ≤20, tag ≤6, source ≤14, bubble ≤10, sound word ≤5, slogan ≤12); `rules.json` (16–40 s, 5–8 shots, opening → districts → finale); `checks.mjs` J1–J18 (4–6 districts, adjacent scenes differ, unique categories, headline has a number, finale brand/CTA copy `meta`, stats appear in `meta.facts`, districts between opening and finale, every price/time appears verbatim in `meta.facts`, headline numbers equal the district count or appear in `meta.facts`, night scenes last, scenes fit the backdrop, old-town travel uses `oldtown`, truncated category names, copied hook patterns, long titles with no pause, a finale with neither stats nor CTA, dates in notices must appear in `meta.facts`, and J18: warn when the kicker reuses the "跟 X 一口气飞越 / 逛完" formula). Industry rules still apply. Self-check: the `district` check frame is taken at beat 3.2 (spec `checkBeat`), where the postcard title and category must be visible (spec `mustShow`); the ticket only names the current stop, never the next one. The city background SVG carries `data-probe-skip`.

**9:16**: `tokens.layout["9:16"]` puts the ticket at y 272, postcards at y 398–674 and 700 wide (text inside x 180–900), mascot at y 968 (1.15×), horizon at y 1580 with road and grass below (a canal in the old town), city scale 1.0; the stamp card starts at y 390 and the mascot lands below its lower-right corner, above y 1340.

**Fits**: products with many clearly separated categories (content platforms, feature-rich software, multi-store or multi-category businesses, course catalogs, a sightseeing route). **Does not fit**: single-benefit or process-heavy products (use cards).
