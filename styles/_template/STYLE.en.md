# __ID__ style (__NAME__) · nine-layer spec

> Status: draft. Based on a breakdown of a ______ short video. No reference footage, stills, characters, brand names, URLs or film clips go into this repo. Every layer needs measured numbers, not adjectives. Method and prompts: `distill/`.

1. **Basics**: aspect, resolution, fps, length, BPM, cuts, voice-over, vertical range used vs our safe area.
2. **Structure**: segment table (time, content, planned shot), information density.
3. **Visual system**: palette with meaning, type scale after applying our font floors, grid and margins, illustration language.
4. **Camera**: movement, parallax, scale changes, transitions.
5. **Motion**: entrance/exit durations, easing, overshoot; what lands on the beat.
6. **Components**: each with fields and length limits, mapped to shots in `template/src/styles/__ID__/shots/`.
7. **Sound**: music sections, effect timeline, sync rules.
8. **Variable vs fixed**: the minimum fields a cheap model fills.
9. **Rules**: everything that can be validated; mark which go into `rules.json` and which into `checks.mjs`.

**Assets to avoid** from the reference, with our replacements. **Good fit / poor fit** for product types.
