# cards style

The default style: a storyboard without `meta.style` uses it. Its code stays where it always was (shots in `template/src/shots/`, themes in `template/src/core/themes.json`, background and caption layers in `template/src/core/layers.tsx`). This page registers it in the nine-layer template so remixers and new-style authors have a reference.

1. **Basics**: 9:16 (1080×1920), 30 fps, 120 BPM by default (one beat = 0.5 s). 15–45 s total (20–30 s is best). Safe area: y 0–205 background only, y 260–540 caption band, y 560–1340 shot area, key content x 180–900.
2. **Structure**: hook (2–3 s, cover title on frame 0) → scene/problem → evidence (dataChart, compare, counter, or meter) → product action/benefit → endCard (4 s). A dataChart organizes a title, takeaway, 1–3 KPIs, chart, annotations, and cited source in one shot. Per-industry structures: `industries/<industry>/recipe.en.md`.
3. **Visual system**: two-color gradient background driven by `mood`, centered paper/light or dark cards (18 themes via `meta.theme`), `meta.brandColor` replaces the accent only. The default warm-emotion theme moves from calm blue through berry to danger red; its lower third uses the clean gradient without floating bubbles, icon silhouettes, or a decorative progress bar. All themes now use clean gradients without lower ambient ornaments. good/warn/bad retain their status roles. dataChart supports bars, lines, dots, stacked bars, and donut charts; Micro is the default series palette, with Mono, Porcelain, Palm, and Wire as alternatives. All themes use clean captions at weight 800 without a heavy outline; warm-emotion uses warm ivory and pale rose; light backgrounds use dark captions, dark backgrounds use light captions, and highlights follow each palette; `{}` marks the highlighted words. The 1080×1920 dataChart type scale is 30 px scope title, 44 px takeaway, 52 px KPI numbers, 30 px legend, and 28 px note/source. Line charts include numeric ticks; dot charts use a shared horizontal scale. Stacked bars are normalized to 100% with stable series and legend colors. The donut center shows the focal category and its share.
4. **Camera**: fixed. Each shot has one moving hero element (needle, number, bubble, tap).
5. **Motion**: spring entrances, key actions on the beat, one new piece of information per beat, at least 0.5 s of stillness at the end. See `template/SHOT_API.md` §6.
6. **Components**: 19 shared shots, fields and length limits in `shots.en.md`.
7. **Sound**: original music generated per film by `scripts/make_bgm.py`; sound effects from each shot spec. Optional voice-over (`meta.voice` + a `vo` per shot, see "Voice-over" in `SKILL.en.md`): narration is synthesized first and shots with `vo` are retimed to it (0.15 s + narration + 0.35 s, whole beats); the caption band lights up word by word with the voice (spoken words solid, the current word in the accent color with a small lift, upcoming words dimmed), at most two lines per page, paged by time; a shot that keeps its `caption` shows the caption and the voice just reads; endCard is voiced without subtitles; the music ducks 10 dB under speech (120 ms down, 300 ms back) and is unchanged elsewhere.
8. **Variable vs fixed**: the model picks shots and writes captions, params, mood and theme; safe area, font floors, transitions and music generation are fixed.
9. **Rules**: see "Hard rules" in `SKILL.en.md` and `industries/<industry>/rules.json`.

**Good fit**: single-benefit products, how-it-works flows, UI demos, physical stores (with industry shots). **Poor fit**: "guess first, then reveal" content (use quiz) or platforms with many categories to show at once (use journey).

## Shared card typography

Shared spacing lives in `template/src/core/cardRhythm.ts`: 40 px horizontal padding for wide cards, 30 px for narrow comparison columns, 32 px section gaps, 24 px regular row gaps, and 16 px dense row gaps. List headings align with the row content; body weight is reduced so readings lead. Comparison headers use smaller icons and 1.35 body line height, with a 64 px gap before the verdict. Dashboard labels sit 12 px above readings, with 24 px between the chart and detail rows. Dense app lists fit row height to the available space, and tap coordinates follow the same layout calculations.

List and product headers use 32–36 px, list body text 40–52 px, comparison body text 36–46 px, key readings 48–76 px, and supporting labels 28–34 px. Long text is fitted to the available space. List headings use the theme's background text color; comparison verdicts use card colors, and editor highlights use accentSoft. Chart colors follow the theme unless an explicit palette is supplied, with dark/light segment text chosen for readability.

## Opening and closing hierarchy

Opening captions lead, with a 36–48 px product hint with a 56 px icon on the themed card surface and no fixed caption strokes. The first frame retains the complete main visual. Closing layouts keep stack, panel and spotlight: centered and brand layouts use a 56–92 px product name and a 64–112 px slogan; panel uses a 40–64 px product name and 64–104 px slogan. Supporting points use 34–46 px text or explicitly packed chips. CTA height is 96 px, using accent/accentText with at least 40 px separation from the supporting group. Chip packing and CTA positioning share the same height calculation.
